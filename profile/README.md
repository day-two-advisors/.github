## 👋 Who we are
 
We're a Carlsbad, CA advisory firm that puts **technology and finance under one roof**, so your architecture, spending and fundraising decisions stay aligned instead of pulling against each other.
 
## 🛠️ What we do
 
| | Technology | Finance |
|---|---|---|
| **Build** | Cloud architecture, infrastructure & IT overhauls | Finance systems that scale with the company |
| **Scale** | Software engineering reviews, delivery practices, hands-on build support | Planning, forecasting, runway and scenario models |
| **Grow** | AI transformation & enablement | Unit economics, pricing and fundraising readiness |
 
**Also:**
 
- 🧭 **Fractional CTO & CFO** — part-time executives on your leadership team, at a fraction of full-time cost
- 🔍 **M&A due diligence for acquirers** — side-by-side review of a target's technology and financials: architecture, security, team, technical debt, controls and earnings quality
- ⚡ **AI Readiness Sprint** — find the AI use cases with real payoff, check your data, and leave with a roadmap and a working prototype
